# YOURS2OURS

**Description**
YOURS2OURS is a community-based platform that connects neighbors through skill exchange and resource sharing. It allows users to offer, borrow, lend, or request skills, spaces, amenities, and supplies while promoting cooperation and stronger community relationships.

**Problem being adressed**
Yours2ours addresses the lack of an organized and accessible platform that allows community members to share, exchange, and access available skills, spaces, amenities, supplies, and other resources. Many individuals possess useful skills and resources that remain unused because they have difficulty finding people who need them or identifying neighbors who can provide the assistance they are looking for. This situation leads to wasted resources, unnecessary expenses, limited access to useful services, and fewer opportunities for community collaboration. Additionally, the absence of a centralized system makes it challenging for people to communicate, arrange exchanges, request resources, and establish trust with other community members. Through NeighborConnect, individuals can easily connect with one another, offer or request available resources, exchange skills, share unused spaces such as yards and rooms, and provide mutual assistance. Ultimately, the project aims to maximize the utilization of existing community resources, reduce unnecessary spending, encourage cooperation, and strengthen relationships among neighbors.

**Problem Objectives**
The general objective of NeighborConnect is to develop a community-based platform that connects individuals and encourages the sharing and exchange of skills, spaces, amenities, supplies, and other available resources. It aims to maximize resource utilization, reduce unnecessary expenses, promote cooperation, and strengthen relationships among community members.

**Users**
1. Community Members – People who offer, borrow, lend, or exchange skills and resources.
2. Resource Providers – Users who share their available skills, spaces, amenities, or supplies.
3. Resource Seekers – Users who need or request resources from other members.
4. System Administrator – Manages user accounts, monitors activities, and maintains the platform.

**Features**
1. User Registration and Profiles – Create accounts and manage personal information.
2. Skill Exchange – Offer or exchange skills such as tutoring, cooking, gardening, and repairs.
3. Space Sharing – Share available rooms, parking spaces, yards, and other areas.
4. Amenity Sharing – Allow community members to share facilities, equipment, and appliances.
5. Supply Sharing – Lend, donate, borrow, or exchange useful items.
6. Request System – Post requests for needed skills, spaces, or resources.
7. User Matching – Connect users based on their offers and requests.
8. Booking and Scheduling – Arrange dates and times for sharing or exchanges.
9. Ratings and Feedback – Rate and review completed transactions.

## Technologies Used

This project is developed using the following web technologies:

- **HTML (HyperText Markup Language)** – Used to structure the website's content, pages, forms, navigation menus, and resource listings.

- **CSS (Cascading Style Sheets)** – Used to design and style the website, including colors, fonts, layouts, and responsive elements to provide a clean and user-friendly interface.

- **JavaScript** – Used to add interactivity and functionality to the website, allowing users to interact with features such as resource listings, search functions, request forms, and other system activities.

## Project Description

NeighborConnect is a community-based platform designed to connect individuals who want to share, exchange, or access available skills, spaces, amenities, supplies, and other resources. It aims to encourage cooperation, reduce unnecessary expenses, maximize resource utilization, and strengthen relationships among community members.

## Main Goal

To provide an accessible and user-friendly platform that makes community resource sharing and skill exchange easier, more organized, and more convenient.

# YOURS2OURS

**Description**
Yours2Ours is a community-based platform designed to connect individuals who want to share, exchange, lend, donate, or access available skills, spaces, amenities, supplies, and other useful resources. The platform encourages community members to help one another by making unused resources more accessible and allowing people to offer their abilities and resources to those who need them.

Through Yours2Ours, users can create profiles, post available resources, request items or services, exchange skills, and connect with other community members. The project aims to promote cooperation, reduce unnecessary expenses, maximize resource utilization, and build stronger relationships within communities.

**Problem being adressed**
Many people in a community possess useful skills, spaces, tools, supplies, and amenities that remain underutilized because others are unaware of their availability. At the same time, individuals who need these resources often struggle to find someone who can provide them. This results in unnecessary expenses, wasted resources, limited access to useful services, and fewer opportunities for community interaction.

Furthermore, the absence of a centralized platform makes it difficult for individuals to offer, request, exchange, and share resources with their neighbors. Yours2Ours aims to address these problems by providing an organized and accessible platform that connects people who have something to offer with those who need it.

**Problem Objectives**
The general objective of Yours2Ours is to develop a community-based platform that connects individuals and encourages the sharing and exchange of skills, spaces, amenities, supplies, and other available resources. It aims to maximize resource utilization, reduce unnecessary expenses, promote cooperation, and strengthen relationships among community members.

**Users**
1. Community Members – People who offer, borrow, lend, or exchange skills and resources.
2. Resource Providers – Users who share their available skills, spaces, amenities, or supplies.
3. Resource Seekers – Users who need or request resources from other members.
4. System Administrator – Manages user accounts, monitors activities, and maintains the platform.

**Features**
1. User Registration and Profiles – Create accounts and manage personal information.
2. Skill Exchange – Offer or exchange skills such as tutoring, cooking, gardening, and repairs.
3. Space Sharing – Share available rooms, parking spaces, yards, and other areas.
4. Amenity Sharing – Allow community members to share facilities, equipment, and appliances.
5. Supply Sharing – Lend, donate, borrow, or exchange useful items.
6. Request System – Post requests for needed skills, spaces, or resources.
7. User Matching – Connect users based on their offers and requests.
8. Booking and Scheduling – Arrange dates and times for sharing or exchanges.
9. Ratings and Feedback – Rate and review completed transactions.

# OOP Concepts Demonstrated

Yours2Ours demonstrates the four fundamental principles of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), which help organize the system, improve code reusability, and make the application easier to maintain.

## 1. Encapsulation

Protects and manages data within classes. For example, the `User` class can contain personal information and account details that are accessed or modified through appropriate methods.

## 2. Inheritance

Allows classes to inherit properties and methods from another class. For example, `Skill`, `Space`, `Amenity`, and `Supply` can inherit common properties from a general `Resource` class.

## 3. Polymorphism

Allows different objects to implement the same method in different ways. For example, different resource types can have their own implementation of a `displayDetails()` method to show their specific information.

## 4. Abstraction

Hides unnecessary implementation details and focuses on essential functionalities. For example, users can request or book resources without needing to understand the internal processes of the system.


## Instructions for Running the Program

Follow these steps to run Yours2Ours locally:

1. Download or clone the project repository.
2. Extract the project folder if it is downloaded as a ZIP file.
3. Open the project folder using Visual Studio Code or another code editor.
4. Locate the `index.html` file.
5. Open `index.html` in your preferred web browser.
6. Alternatively, install the **Live Server** extension in Visual Studio Code, right-click `index.html`, and select **Open with Live Server**.
7. Explore the available pages and features of Yours2Ours.


## Known Limitations

The current version of Yours2Ours may have the following limitations:

- The platform may have limited functionality depending on the implemented features.
- User matching may rely on basic search and filtering rather than advanced recommendation algorithms.
- Resource availability depends on the information provided by users.
- User verification and security features may be limited.
- Real-time messaging and notifications may not yet be available.
- The platform may require additional development to support a larger number of users.
